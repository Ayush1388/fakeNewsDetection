    About the model

    GE-Stack looks at three things about a tweet and lets a final model decide how much to trust each.

    The three views

    Text
    The wording of the tweet.
    Spreaders
    Which accounts retweeted or replied to it.
    Cascade
    How big the spread was and how quickly it happened.

    What it can and cannot tell you

    It sorts a tweet into one of four categories: not a rumour, true, false or unverified. It learned this from about a thousand labelled tweets per dataset. It does not look up sources or check facts.

    On tweets about stories it had seen, accuracy is about 92%. On stories it had never seen, it falls to roughly 70 to 86% depending on how strictly stories are separated, and to about 57 to 65% when predicting newer tweets from older ones. Treat a ruling as a lead to follow up, not as proof.

    Without spread data, only the text view is used, which is less accurate.
